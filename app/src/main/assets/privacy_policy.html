Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 14, 2026

Welcome to BeeTalk Lite ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a functional dating experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identity: Name, email address, gender, and date of birth provided during registration.
  • Profile Information: Dating preferences, bio, and interests.
  • Media Content: Photos you voluntarily upload to your public profile.
  • Location Data: With your explicit consent, we collect precise GPS location data to show you nearby matches.
  • Communication Data: We process and store messages sent between you and other users to facilitate the chat service.

2. Third-Party Services & Data Integration

Our App integrates several third-party SDKs to improve functionality and support our operations:

A. Advertising (AdMob & AppLovin)

We use Google AdMob and AppLovin to serve advertisements. These services may collect and use your Android Advertising ID to provide personalized ads and measure ad performance. You can manage your ad preferences in your device settings.

B. Analytics & Stability (Google Firebase)

We use Firebase for the following purposes:

  • Google Analytics: To analyze user behavior and app engagement.
  • Crashlytics: To monitor app performance and identify technical issues or crashes.
  • Cloud Messaging: To send you real-time notifications about matches and messages.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to:

  • Create and maintain your user account.
  • Provide accurate matchmaking based on location and preferences.
  • Enable real-time messaging between users.
  • Monitor and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity.
  • Personalize your experience through targeted content and advertisements.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

We retain your data only as long as your account is active. You have full control over your data:

Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the app by navigating to:
Settings > Account Settings > Danger Zone > Delete My Account.

Upon deletion, your profile, photos, matches, and chat history will be permanently removed from our active servers.

5. Children's Privacy

Bee Talk Lite is intended strictly for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under 18. If we discover a minor has created an account, we will delete their information immediately.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, please be aware that no transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
